## 📰 Recent Cybersecurity Incident I Read About

### French Supermarket Refrigeration Cyberattack — October 2026

In October 2026, during the night of **October 11–12**, attackers remotely interfered with refrigeration-control systems at hundreds of French supermarkets. Several major retail chains were affected, and reports described attackers manipulating controller settings and triggering the built-in **defrost function**. As a result, refrigeration temperatures rose, fresh and frozen food spoiled, and the affected businesses suffered financial losses. French authorities, including CERT-FR (part of the national cybersecurity agency ANSSI), advised operators to disconnect the affected controllers from the internet or strictly filter access to them. At the time of the reports, neither the attackers' identities nor their exact motive had been confirmed by reliable sources.

### Why This Incident Interested Me

- It showed me that cybersecurity affects **real-world equipment**, not just websites and computers. A fridge in a supermarket can be a target too.
- It made clear that **poorly secured or outdated connected systems** can cause real physical and financial damage, like spoiled food and lost revenue.
- It highlighted three important lessons: the importance of **secure remote access**, keeping systems **updated with timely security patches**, and properly **protecting Internet-connected devices** instead of leaving them exposed.
